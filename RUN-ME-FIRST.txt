LATEST CHANGES IN THIS SNAPSHOT
- Frontpage/Job Opportunities cards: the expanded quick view is slimmed to
  just the "Job description" block — the Item No. / Vacancies / Salary
  Grade / Monthly Salary / Published / Deadline facts grid is gone (the
  complete facts still live in the desktop detail pane and on the posting
  page). On phones the card summary is no longer tappable; the expansion
  ends in a "View Details" button that opens the complete posting page
  (#/job-opportunities?job=<id>) — mobile view only, desktop keeps the
  card-tap → side pane flow.
- Applicant home on PHONES: the mini profile card is replaced by a
  brand-blue promo banner ("See how you can find a job Quickly") with a
  white "Read more" pill that routes to the Job Opportunities board —
  mobile view only; desktop keeps the identity card with the metrics.
  Banner art is the user-supplied applicant-at-laptop vector
  (public/home-banner-vector.png) with its paper background keyed out,
  sized back to the previous cutout's compact footprint after review
  (the AI-generated worker cutout was dropped).
- Job cards on the frontpage and the Job Opportunities board: the
  "Read full description" link in the inline quick view is gone —
  selecting a card already shows the complete posting in the adjacent
  pane (the home Apply Now pane keeps its link, since it deep-links to
  the dedicated posting page). The Applied pill and deadline warning
  still render in the quick view when they apply.
- "Vacant Positions" page fully retired: the Job Opportunities explorer
  is the canonical board at #/job-opportunities and the complete posting
  page lives at #/job-opportunities?job=<id> (the "Read full description"
  destination). Legacy #/vacant-positions and #/jobs links alias onto the
  board, so old bookmarks keep working.
- Frontpage decluttered: the posting pane's "View all jobs" link is gone
  — the landing IS the job board, so the link only looped back to the
  same content (mobile keeps its "Back to job list" affordance).
- Mobile Profile polish: the Personal Information sub-parts (Identity /
  Address / Legal) render as ONE card on phones — no more box-in-a-box
  double insets squeezing the fields; Character References keeps its
  per-reference entry cards.
- Section-header buttons compacted on phones: "Edit Profile", "Done
  Editing", "Cancel" and "Save Changes" shrink to a tidy 36px pill on
  mobile and keep the exact 44px desktop anatomy from sm up.
- Profile wizard footer on mobile: "Back to …" / "Continue to …" now
  share one compact row of equal halves with truncating labels (no more
  oversized wrapping pills); solo Continue takes the full row.
- Job detail page (#/job-opportunities?job=<id>) rebuilt to the pinned
  Workze-style reference: typographic title/employer/meta column, kept
  "Job Description | Job Detail" tab navs, sticky summary card with the
  dark "Apply for this job" CTA + agency card.
- Applicant Profile: redundant header "Add" buttons removed from the
  Education / Work Experience / Training / Eligibility / Awards sections
  (the in-form "Add additional entry" slot is the single add path).
- "Mark Profile as Complete" moved to the right of the "My Profile"
  section header (disabled until required sections are filled; green
  "Profile Complete" pill after marking).
- PDS completion gate: once the required profile sections are filled,
  Home and the Jobs pages redirect back to the profile with an
  explanatory toast until "Mark Profile as Complete" is clicked.
- Router fix: #/profile and #/account-settings render their own views
  (a jobs-board dispatch regression no longer clobbers them).
